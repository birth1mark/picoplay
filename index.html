Quick Start Guide

Get up and running in under 2 minutes on your platform of choice.

Installing on PS4 via GoldHEN Package Installer

  1. Download the Fake PKG: Get picoplay-ps4.pkg from GitHub Releases.
  2. Option A — Via GoldHEN FTP (Fastest): Connect to your PS4 console via FTP on port 2121 and upload the PKG to: /data/pkg/picoplay-ps4.pkg
  3. Option B — Via USB Flash Drive: Copy picoplay-ps4.pkg to the root directory of a FAT32 or exFAT formatted USB drive and plug it into your PS4.
  4. Install on Console: On your PS4, navigate to Settings → GoldHEN → Package Installer and install PicoPlay.
  5. Launch & Play: Launch PicoPlay from the PlayStation Home Menu! Use Options for Pause/Menu, Touchpad or Right Stick for mouse control, and L1 + R1 + Options to quit.

Installing on Android Smartphones & Handhelds

  1. Download APK: Download picoplay-full-v1.0.2.apk directly to your Android device.
  2. Allow Unknown Sources: If prompted by Android, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager.
  3. Install & Grant Storage: Open the downloaded APK and tap Install.
  4. Load Cartridges: Tap into the built-in BBS store to download cartridges instantly, or place your existing .p8 / .p8.png files into your device storage!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PicoPlay and PICO-8 cart compatibility.

What is PicoPlay?
PicoPlay is a free and open-source recreation of an 8-bit fantasy console engine. It allows gamers and developers to play community cartridges natively on modern hardware like the PlayStation 4 and Android with rock-solid 60 FPS performance, low input latency, and controller integration.
Do I need to own PICO-8 to play games?
No! Thousands of PICO-8 cartridges published on the Lexaloffle BBS are free and open for public play. PicoPlay includes an integrated BBS browser that lets you discover, download, and enjoy these community games directly inside the app.
How does DualShock 4 mouse emulation work on PS4?
Games with mouse input (such as Slipways) read PICO-8 hardware registers stat(32), stat(33), and stat(34). PicoPlay PS4 reads the DualShock 4 touchpad coordinates and right analog stick directly, mapping them smoothly to the 128×128 fantasy screen with trigger clicks for left/right mouse buttons.
Is PicoPlay open source?
Yes, PicoPlay is 100% open source under permissive licensing. Both the PlayStation 4 source code and the Android source code are maintained publicly on GitHub by birth1mark.
